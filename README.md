A public archive documenting research, manuscript versions, mathematical investigations, and supporting materials concerning the **putative Conway 99-graph** and strongly regular graphs with parameters \((99,14,1,2)\).

## 🔬 Research Overview

This project investigates structural and automorphism-related constraints for a hypothetical strongly regular graph with parameters \((99,14,1,2)\).

Topics explored in the manuscript include:

- Triangle-orbit congruences under prime-order automorphisms.
- Spectral projectors associated with the graph's eigenvalues.
- Constraints involving automorphisms of order three.
- Fixed-point and orbit-counting arguments.
- Involution-related restrictions under stated classification assumptions.
- Quotient-matrix identities and computational checks.

The work studies necessary structural constraints; it **does not establish whether the Conway 99-graph exists**.

## 🧪 Verification and Research Status

This is an exploratory mathematical research project. The manuscript includes computational checks and mathematical arguments, but these should not be treated as independently established results without further review.

**Important limitations:**

- The mathematical claims have not yet been independently verified in full.
- Computational checks do not, by themselves, prove every mathematical claim.
- Some arguments rely on external classification results whose precise references require further checking.
- The existence question for a strongly regular graph with parameters \((99,14,1,2)\) remains unresolved by this work.
- Publication or repository availability does not imply peer review, mathematical correctness, or acceptance by a journal.

The archive should document corrections, unresolved questions, and independent verification as they occur.
